How can we help?

Answers to the questions we get most. Still stuck? Email us and a real person replies.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Habibi Gains. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date and you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so Apple decides refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but Premium isn't showing.

Open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

What happens if I lose my streak?

You have 48 hours to restore it. Premium members get two free restores a month, and anyone can buy a single restore.

An invite link didn't open the app.

Make sure Habibi Gains is installed and up to date, then tap the link again. If it still doesn't open, paste the invite code shown on the page into the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. You can delete after 30 days, so you can change your mind, or delete everything straight away. Want a copy first? Tap Download my data in Settings.

Is Habibi Gains on Android?

Not yet. The Android version is coming soon on Google Play.
