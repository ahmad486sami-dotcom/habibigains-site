Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest):

  1. Open Habibi Gains and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Choose to delete after 30 days (you can change your mind) or delete everything immediately.

Cannot open the app? Email hello@habibigains.store from the email address on your account and write "Delete my account". We answer within one month.

What gets deleted: your profile, food and weight logs, photos, friends, challenges and notifications. Backups clear within a further 30 days.

Want a copy first? In the app, go to Settings and tap Download my data.
