Be decent

  • No harassment, hate, threats, nudity, spam, or shaming people about their bodies or what they eat.
  • Do not post photos or information about other people without their permission.
  • Do not try to break, overload, copy or scrape the app or its food data.

Your content

  • What you add (photos, foods, logs) stays yours. You let us store and show it only so the app works, and only to the people your privacy settings allow.
  • Foods you create or correct may be checked by our team and, once approved, shown to other users without your name.

Food data

  • Food information comes from public databases, users and our own estimates. It can be wrong. Check the label when it matters.

Challenges and stakes

  • Challenge "stakes" (like "loser buys shawarma") are between friends. We do not handle money or enforce them.
